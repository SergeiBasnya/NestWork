# Code of Conduct

NestWork contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming,
constructive and professional.

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while remaining factual.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or expose private information
  about another participant.
- Respect licenses, authorship and the confidentiality of security reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
these expectations are repeatedly or seriously violated. Report conduct issues
privately to `contact@nestwork.site` with the subject `Code of conduct`.

# Product

## Register

brand

(NestWork the app is a product surface; this file's default register is **brand**
because the active design work is the public landing page at `/`. App-shell
surfaces under `/workspace/*` can override to `product` per task.)

## Users

Small remote and hybrid teams who used to share an office and feel the loss of it:
the spontaneous "got a sec?", overhearing a problem and jumping in, the coffee-corner
chat. Decision-maker is usually a founder or team lead choosing a daily hub for 2 to
15 people. They evaluate the landing in under a minute, often skeptical of "virtual
office" tools that felt gimmicky.

## Product Purpose

NestWork is a 2D virtual HQ. Your avatar walks around a space; when you get close to a
colleague the video and audio open on their own, and fade when you walk away. Rooms,
chat, presence and statuses round it out. The landing's job: make a visitor feel the
spontaneity in a few seconds and click through to sign in. Success = sign-ins from the
public page once the product opens beyond the private crash-test accounts.

## Brand Personality

Warm, spontaneous, human. The "Ruche" (beehive) metaphor runs through everything:
honey gold, warm charcoal, hexagon/honeycomb cells, little pixel-art teammates moving
in a shared space. Playful but a credible work tool, never childish or corporate.

## Anti-references

- Cold enterprise SaaS landings (navy-to-violet gradients, abstract 3D blobs, stock
  "diverse team at laptops" photography).
- Zoom/Meet grid-of-rectangles video aesthetic. NestWork is the opposite of a scheduled
  call: presence is ambient, not a meeting.
- Sterile dashboard chrome. This page should feel like a place, not a control panel.

## Design Principles

- **Show presence, not a feature list.** Lead with the feeling of being in the same
  room; the bullet points come after.
- **The hive made tangible.** Hexagon cells, honey light and the real in-app pixel
  avatars carry the brand; warmth comes from craft, not from stock photos.
- **Honest pixel-craft.** Use the actual game sprites and the product's own video-bubble
  shapes. The page should look made by the people who made the app.
- **Earn the click in one fold.** A skeptical lead should get it before scrolling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (body text ≥4.5:1; the honey-on-white traps are real, keep body copy
toward the charcoal end of the ramp). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for the floating
and reveal animations. Copy is French (tutoiement), matching the app.
